Supervision and resource policy
Advanced supervision and data retention

Numeric record limits exclude checkpoint, calibration workspace and recovery snapshot files. Inspect host storage before large calibration jobs.

Automatic mode requests a checkpoint-safe stop; it never force-kills detector training. A stalled process may be unable to save. Advisory mode is the default.

Calibration workload and manual jobs

Calibration uses up to 32 copied training images and temporary model state. It does not change active weights. Jobs wait until training and video analysis are idle.

Historical checkpoints use recorded metrics; missing memory data is excluded from safety learning.

Independent schedules

Schedules default off. Busy periods defer up to two hours, then skip. Missed executions do not run in a burst. Adviser promotion always requires a separate decision.

Jobs and calibration history

Adviser versions and rollback

Resource-safety retraining requires at least 100 benchmark windows from six independent runs/calibrations. Historical duration candidates use at least 20 epochs from two runs; they remain experimental and cannot be promoted as safety predictors. A candidate must pass held-out evaluation before promotion; unfamiliar hardware uses rules.

3
Build and keep the result

VAOS trains a new checkpoint, saves a dated copy of the existing best.pt, then installs the successful result as best.pt.

ⓘ What will this operation transfer?

It fine-tunes the selected YOLO checkpoint on reviewed camera samples, optionally combined with a validated COCO dataset. It does not merge hidden knowledge from unrelated models. The existing best.pt is backed up before a successful result replaces it. Imported .pt files should come from a source you trust.
