CUSTOM MODEL WORKFLOW

Build or update your best.pt

Train a custom detector using a model you choose, public COCO examples, and the live detections you have confirmed.

1
Choose a starting model

YOLO26m is the recommended starting point. You can use your current best.pt or add another trusted YOLO model.

2
Choose learning material

Choose reviewed camera samples alone or add them to a validated public dataset.

3
Build and keep the result

VAOS trains a new checkpoint, saves a dated copy of the existing best.pt, then installs the successful result as best.pt.

ⓘ What will this operation transfer?

It fine-tunes the selected YOLO checkpoint on reviewed camera samples, optionally combined with a validated COCO dataset. It does not merge hidden knowledge from unrelated models. The existing best.pt is backed up before a successful result replaces it. Imported .pt files should come from a source you trust.
