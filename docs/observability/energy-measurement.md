# Energy measurement and provenance

VAOS records CPU, RAM and visible GPU utilization through the existing collector. Utilization is not energy and is never converted to watts. Where `nvidia-smi` provides a finite `power.draw` value, VAOS now records the measured NVIDIA GPU board-power reading separately.

GPU energy is estimated by trapezoidal integration between successive measured board-power samples. Only gaps between 20 and 90 seconds are integrated; shorter duplicate samples are ignored and longer gaps are skipped and recorded. The lifetime overview accumulates fractional Wh per device, while the lifetime metric counter reports whole-Wh increments. Recent interval observations are retained for 30 days. The metric metadata marks integrated energy as estimated; the raw power readings are marked measured. If no sensor value is available, the dashboard reports energy as unavailable rather than inferring it from utilization or a GPU model specification.

The sampler hashes the NVIDIA device UUID with the persistent installation identity before storage; it never persists the raw UUID. Power is GPU-board-only, not wall or whole-system energy. VAOS does not assign energy to a training job because the existing sampler cannot prove which job used the device. `coverage_seconds` reports integrated intervals, and `skipped_gap_count` reports detected long gaps. A hard process exit or clock discontinuity can still leave an unmeasured gap; no energy is fabricated for that time. CPU/system energy and wall-socket energy remain unavailable unless a separately validated sensor supplies them.
